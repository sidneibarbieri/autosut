## Claim Boundaries

- **Not claimed:** faithful or historically equivalent campaign replay. No ground-truth historical environment or trace equivalence study.
- **Not claimed:** automatic reconstruction for arbitrary ATT&CK campaigns. The executable action catalog and evaluated campaign slice are bounded.
- **Not claimed:** measured analyst-effort reduction. No controlled human-subject or time-on-task experiment.
- **Not claimed:** scalability or minimal-overhead result. No scaling curve, resource study, or comparative overhead experiment.
- **Not claimed:** validated accuracy of CF/VMR/ID routing labels. The released manual adjudication packet remains unlabeled.

## Reproduction

```bash
bash run_review_check.sh
```

The machine-readable version is `release/claim_evidence_traceability.json`.
Claim identifiers and measurement-row prefixes are stable historical keys,
not manuscript question numbers. The `evidence_dimension` field groups
results by scientific subject independently of manuscript organization.
Engineering witnesses support only their stated scope; they do not
replace controlled consequence experiments.

The detailed historical rows remain in
`measurement/sut/scripts/results/claim_evidence_map.csv`.
